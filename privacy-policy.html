» PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PCM Productions handles information in connection with games published by PCM Productions.

Information We Collect

PCM Productions does not ask players to provide sensitive personal information such as passwords, financial information, government identification numbers, or precise location information through our games.

Depending on the game and the features enabled, our games may use third-party services that automatically process certain information. This may include information such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, app interactions, gameplay activity, diagnostics, crash information, and other technical information.

The specific information processed depends on the services and features used by an individual game.

Analytics

Some of our games may use analytics services to understand how players interact with the game. This may include information such as gameplay progression, feature usage, performance, and crashes.

We use analytics information to understand how our games are used and to improve their quality, stability, performance, and gameplay experience.

Advertising

Some of our games may display advertisements using third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may automatically collect or process certain information, such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, and diagnostic information, for purposes including providing advertisements, measuring advertising performance, fraud prevention, and improving their services.

Advertising practices are also subject to the privacy policies and terms of the relevant third-party advertising providers.

Google Play Games Services

Some of our games may support Google Play Games Services, including features such as player authentication, achievements, or leaderboards.

When these features are enabled, Google may process information associated with your Google Play Games account and your use of these features. This processing is subject to Google's privacy practices.

In-App Purchases

Some of our games may offer optional in-app purchases.

Purchases are processed through the applicable platform's billing system, such as Google Play Billing. PCM Productions does not receive or store your complete payment card details through our games.

Third-Party Services

Our games may use third-party services to provide functionality, analytics, advertising, authentication, crash reporting, or purchases.

Depending on the game, these services may include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Games Services
  • Firebase services
  • Google Play Billing

Not every game published by PCM Productions uses all of these services. The services actually used may vary between games.

Data Sharing and Sale

PCM Productions does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when those services are used to provide advertising, analytics, authentication, crash reporting, purchases, or other game functionality.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled directly by PCM Productions. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

PCM Productions does not intentionally retain personal information longer than necessary for the purposes for which it is processed. Third-party services used by our games may retain information according to their own policies and retention practices.

Children's Privacy

Our games are not intended to encourage children to provide sensitive personal information to PCM Productions.

Where a game is intended for or made available to children or families, we will comply with the applicable platform requirements and laws concerning children's privacy.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our games, services, or data practices change. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
